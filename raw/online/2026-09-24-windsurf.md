## Ключевые тезисы (кратко)

- Agentic VS Code fork от Codeium, ребрендирован в **Devin Desktop** 2 июня 2026 (после покупки Cognition).
- Cascade (plan-then-execute), Memories, Flow awareness, Codemaps, SWE-1.6 (собственная модель).
- SOC 2 Type II, FedRAMP High, self-host — сильнейший enterprise-стек.
- Pro $20/мес, Max $200/мес, Enterprise custom.

## Ключевые цитаты

> Windsurf is an agentic VS Code fork that out-paces Cursor on workflow feel, dragged down by a year of ownership chaos and an overnight pricing flip.

> Cascade operates as a stateful agent with a plan-then-execute loop — you give it a goal, it decomposes the goal into sequenced steps, you approve or trim the plan, and only then does it begin writing code.

> The standout capability is what Windsurf calls "Flow awareness." Cascade tracks everything you do - files you edit, terminal commands you run, clipboard contents, conversation history - and uses this shared timeline to infer your intent.
